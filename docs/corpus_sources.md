# Telugu Municipal Complaint Corpus: Source Audit

Audit date: 2026-10-15
Research target: Authentic Telugu complaints concerning Hyderabad municipal services
Project: GrievanceHub

## 1. GHMC Official Website

URL: https://www.ghmc.gov.in/
Status: Candidate source; complaint corpus availability unverified.

Evidence:
- GHMC documents a grievance redressal system.
- Its website provides grievance-related information and application forms.
- A published General Grievances form documents the fields collected.
- The existence of a grievance system does not establish that complaint
  text is available for bulk download or research reuse.

References:
- https://www.ghmc.gov.in/howdoi.aspx
- https://www.ghmc.gov.in/CSC_Applications/General_Grievances.pdf
- https://www.ghmc.gov.in/terms_conditions.aspx

Next action:
Ask GHMC whether an anonymized historical complaint dataset is available
for academic research, including records originally submitted in Telugu.

Do not scrape restricted systems or attempt to access private complaint
records without authorization.

## 2. Telangana Open Data Portal

URL: https://data.telangana.gov.in/
Status: Candidate source; relevant dataset not yet verified.

Next action:
Search the catalog for municipal grievances, GHMC complaints,
sanitation, roads, drainage, streetlights, and water services.
Inspect each candidate dataset's metadata, language, license, and
download availability before accepting it.

## 3. Open Government Data Platform India

URL: https://www.data.gov.in/
Status: Additional discovery source.

Next action:
Search for Hyderabad and Telangana municipal complaint datasets.
Confirm the publishing organization, original text language, license,
and whether records contain complaint narratives or only aggregate statistics.

## 4. IIIT Hyderabad Sentiraama Corpus

URL: https://ltrc.iiit.ac.in/showfile.php?filename=downloads%2Fsentiraama%2F
Status: Supplementary Telugu NLP resource; not a municipal complaint corpus.

Potential use:
Investigate permitted use for general Telugu language processing.
Do not mix these reviews with authentic municipal complaints.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria for a Primary Dataset

A candidate dataset is accepted only after verifying:
- Authentic source and collection provenance
- Original Telugu text or an explicitly documented language distribution
- Relevance to Hyderabad municipal services
- Permission for academic research and intended model development
- Privacy safeguards and appropriate redaction
- Available metadata and known sampling limitations

## 6. Current Conclusion

No source has yet been verified as providing a reusable corpus of
authentic Telugu GHMC complaint narratives.

The immediate priority is authorized source discovery and acquisition,
not model training.

## 7. Decision Log

Do not label translated or synthetic records as authentic submissions.
Do not treat a complaint form, status lookup, or aggregate dashboard
as evidence that complaint narratives are available for download.
