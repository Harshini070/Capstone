# Telugu GHMC Corpus: Source Verification Report

Audit date: 2026-10-15
Target: Original Telugu municipal complaint narratives from Hyderabad

## Finding 1: GHMC Official Website

URL: https://www.ghmc.gov.in/

Status: Official grievance system verified; reusable corpus not verified.

The official website documents its grievance-redressal system. The
existence of this service does not establish that historical complaint
narratives are downloadable or available for research reuse.

References:
- https://www.ghmc.gov.in/howdoi.aspx
- https://www.ghmc.gov.in/CSC_Applications/General_Grievances.pdf

Action:
Request information about authorized access to anonymized historical
complaint records, original language, available metadata, and reuse terms.

## Finding 2: Telangana Open Data

URL: https://data.telangana.gov.in/

Status: Candidate catalog; suitable corpus not verified.

Action:
Search the catalog and record actual dataset URLs, metadata, language,
download availability, license, and provenance.

## Finding 3: India Open Government Data

URL: https://www.data.gov.in/

Status: Additional discovery channel.

Grievance-related aggregate statistics exist, but no suitable native-Telugu
GHMC complaint-text dataset has been verified in this audit.

Action:
Continue searching for Hyderabad-specific municipal complaint narratives.
Do not substitute aggregate counts for complaint text.

## Privacy Requirements

The published GHMC general grievance form requests identifying details.
Any research acquisition must address data minimization, anonymization,
access restrictions, retention, and permitted reuse before processing.

## Decision

No verified primary Telugu GHMC complaint corpus has been acquired.

The next priority is authorized data access and further source verification.
Model training must wait until the corpus and its provenance are established.
