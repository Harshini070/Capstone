# Corpus Collection Protocol

## Objective
Develop an authentic, documented Telugu-language corpus of municipal
complaints relevant to Hyderabad and GHMC.

## Inclusion Criteria
- Original complaint text is available in Telugu.
- The record concerns a relevant municipal service or issue.
- Source provenance and acquisition method are documented.
- Research access and intended reuse are permitted.

## Exclusion Criteria
- Translations misrepresented as original Telugu submissions.
- Synthetic records misrepresented as real complaints.
- Unrelated records or records without sufficient provenance.
- Records whose usage restrictions prohibit the intended research.

## Required Provenance
Record source organization, URL, acquisition date, collection method,
applicable terms, original format, and known limitations.

## Validation
Assess language, encoding, duplicates, missing text, schema consistency,
category quality, and personally identifiable information.

## Research Integrity
Telugu-script presence alone does not establish authenticity.
